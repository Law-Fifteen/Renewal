✎ Pre-Call Preparation

Before dialing, ensure the ISR is mentally prepared and informed. Great calls start before the phone rings.

CRM & Account Review

  • CRM reviewed and previous interactions understood
  • Lead source and campaign context understood
  • Previous objections or concerns noted
  • Decision-maker(s) identified
  • Property details reviewed (age, type, prior work)

Call Objective

  • Clear objective defined for this call
  • Desired outcome identified (appointment, follow-up, referral)
  • Potential objections anticipated
  • Key talking points prepared

Mental Preparation

  • Energy and tone set for the call
  • Confidence level assessed
  • Focused on outcomes, not scripts

👁 Live Call Observation

Use this checklist while sitting beside the ISR or listening to recordings. Check each item as you observe it.

Preparation

  • CRM reviewed, notes understood, objective clear
  • ISR prepared before dialing

Opening & Permission

  • Confident, professional tone from first word
  • Earned the right to continue (energy, clarity)
  • Asked for time naturally and respectfully
  • Prospect felt respected, not ambushed

Rapport & Discovery

  • Personal connection established without wasting time
  • Conversational tone maintained
  • Asked meaningful questions before pitching
  • Uncovered a real problem or need
  • Identified decision-makers early

Active Listening & Value Positioning

  • Did not interrupt the prospect
  • Acknowledged and validated responses
  • Tailored conversation to prospect's needs
  • Connected Andersen's value to customer's specific situation
  • Sold outcomes instead of windows

Objection Handling

  • Stayed calm, empathetic, and confident
  • Explored the objection before responding
  • Diagnosed the root concern (money, trust, timing, fear)
  • Educated without arguing or pressuring

Trial Close & Appointment Close

  • Checked for buying signals (trial close)
  • Tested readiness before pushing forward
  • Made a clear, confident ask for the appointment
  • Secured commitment (date, time, decision-makers)

Calendar Confirmation & CRM

  • Date, time, and location confirmed
  • All decision-makers confirmed to attend
  • Appointment is likely to hold
  • CRM notes are accurate and complete
  • Disposition and next steps documented
  • Another employee could understand the interaction from notes

💬 Post-Call Coaching Debrief

Use this structure after each observed call. Self-assessment first, then coaching.

Self-Assessment

Ask the ISR

"How do you think that call went?"

Let them evaluate themselves before you offer feedback. This builds self-awareness and trust.

Strengths

Reinforce 2-3 behaviors to continue

Be specific. Vague praise doesn't change behavior.

Biggest Opportunity

Focus on the single highest-impact improvement

One focused improvement beats ten scattered corrections.

Live Role Play

Practice that portion immediately

Have the ISR re-do the objection, the opening, or the close right now. Muscle memory beats memorization.

Next 10 Calls Commitment

One Measurable Behavior

Agree on one specific behavior to implement on the next 10 calls. Make it observable and measurable.
